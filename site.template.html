Dust Out

Snap your fingers on a video call and crumble into dust. A minute later, you're back.

Free and open source. Works in Chrome on Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. No AI, no accounts, and nothing leaves your computer except the video your meeting already sends. Source on GitHub.

1. Install it

Recommended

Chrome extension

Download the extension
  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called dust-out-extension.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (paste it into the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the dust-out-extension folder.
  5. Optional: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin Dust Out.

That's it. It switches itself on in Teams and Google Meet.

If you can't install extensions

Bookmark

Dust Out
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Cmd+Shift+B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows.
  2. Drag the Dust Out button above onto the bookmarks bar. Don't click it here; it only works inside a meeting.
  3. In your meeting tab, click the bookmark on the screen before you join.

Works on Teams. Google Meet blocks bookmarks from running, so use the extension there. You click the bookmark again each time you reload the meeting page.

2. Use it in a call

  1. Open your meeting in Chrome (not the desktop app) and join with your camera on. The Dust Out card appears at the bottom left.
  2. Click Save empty room, then step fully out of view for 5 seconds. Check that the Saved empty room picture has no one in it, then sit back down.
  3. Snap your fingers, or press Option+Shift+V (Alt+Shift+V on Windows). Stay seated for about 3 seconds while you crumble.
  4. You're gone. Everyone sees the empty room. Come back within a minute: the dust flies back and you reform on its own, or snap again to return sooner.

What others see on the card is exactly what your call is sending, so you can always check it's working. Keep your camera, lights and background filter the same as when you saved the room.

3. Snap for real (optional)

By sound

Tick Listen for a finger snap, click Teach my snap and snap 3 times, a second apart. It learns your snap on your mic and remembers it.

By hand gesture

Click Hand gesture and allow the camera when asked. Snap with your hand in view. With the bookmark this needs a small helper running on your computer; the extension doesn't.

Something's not working?

The card never appears

Make sure the meeting is open in Chrome, not the desktop app, and that your camera is on. With the extension, the card shows once your camera joins a call; you can also click the Dust Out toolbar button and choose Show the card. With the bookmark, click it before joining.

I snap and nothing happens

Save the empty room first. Then try the shortcut Option+Shift+V. If the shortcut works but snapping doesn't, use Teach my snap again in a quiet moment.

The card's preview shows the dust, but my own video doesn't

Others still see it. Some meeting layouts show your self-view from a separate copy. Click Copy diagnostics on the card and open an issue with it.

It says "Couldn't find you"

The saved empty room had you in it, or the camera moved. Click Save empty room again and step fully out of view.

My company laptop won't let me turn on Developer mode

Use the bookmark instead (Teams only).

Still stuck? Open an issue and paste what Copy diagnostics gives you.

Privacy

Dust Out has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It only changes your own outgoing video, only on Teams, Google Meet and sites you turn on yourself. The microphone and camera are used only while you've switched on snap detection, and only on your computer.
