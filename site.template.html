Extension

  1. Downloaddust-out-extension.zip, then unzip it.
  2. Open extensionsPaste chrome://extensions into Chrome's address bar.
  3. Developer modeTurn on the switch in the top right corner.
  4. Load unpackedClick it and choose the dust-out-extension folder.

Done. Reload any open meeting tabs. It turns itself on in Teams and Google Meet; for other sites, click its toolbar button and choose Turn on for this site.

Bookmark

Dust Out (Teams only)

Teams only. Google Meet blocks bookmarks, so clicking it there does nothing. On Meet, use the extension or the console snippet.

Show your bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B, or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows) and drag the button onto it. In your Teams meeting, click it on the screen before you join.

Console snippet

Chrome's developer console can run Dust Out where extensions are blocked, including on Google Meet. Save it once, then start it before each call.

Once

  1. Click Copy the code.
  2. Press Cmd+Option+J (Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows), then Cmd+Shift+P. Type snippet and choose Create new snippet.
  3. Right-click it, Rename it to Dust Out, paste the code and press Cmd+S. If Chrome blocks the paste, type allow pasting first.

Each call

  1. Open the meeting and stop on the screen before you join.
  2. Press Cmd+Option+J, open the Dust Out snippet in the Sources tab and press Cmd+Enter. Or paste the code into the Console and press Enter.
  3. The card appears. Join. On Meet, turn your camera off and on once if the card asks.

It runs only in that tab until you reload. It's still code your IT team hasn't approved, so use your judgement on a work laptop.

Use it in a call

Open the meeting in Chrome, not the desktop app, and join with your camera on. The Dust Out card appears at the bottom left.

  1. Save the empty room. Click Save empty room and step fully out of view for five seconds. Check the saved picture has no one in it, then sit back down.
  2. Snap. Snap your fingers, or press Option+Shift+V (Alt+Shift+V on Windows). Stay seated for about three seconds while you crumble.
  3. Leave. Everyone sees the empty room. The dust flies back after the time you pick under Come back after on the card (30 seconds to 5 minutes, or When I snap), or snap again to come back sooner.

What others see on the card is exactly what your call sends, so you can always check it's working. Keep your camera, lights and background filter as they were when you saved the room.

Snap for real

The shortcut always works. These let an actual finger snap do it.

By sound

Tick Listen for a finger snap, then Teach my snap and snap three times. It learns how your snap sounds on your mic and ignores talking and typing.

By hand

Click Hand gesture and allow the camera, then snap with your hand in view. Handy when you're muted or the room is loud. Extension only: the bookmark and console snippet don't have it, so snap by sound or use the shortcut there.

Something's not working?

The card has a Copy diagnostics link. If none of these help, open an issue and paste what it copies.

The card never appears

Make sure the meeting is open in Chrome, not the desktop app, and your camera is on. With the extension, the card shows once your camera joins a call, or click the toolbar button and choose Show the card. With the bookmark, click it before joining.

I clicked the bookmark on Google Meet and nothing happened

That's expected: Google Meet blocks bookmarks from running any code, so the bookmark can't work there. Use the extension, or the console snippet if extensions are blocked.

I snap and nothing happens

Save the empty room first, then try Option+Shift+V. If the shortcut works but snapping doesn't, run Teach my snap again in a quiet moment.

The card shows the dust, but my own video doesn't

Everyone else still sees it. Some meeting layouts show your self-view from a separate copy. Copy the diagnostics and open an issue so it can be fixed for that layout.

It says "Couldn't find you"

The saved empty room had you in it, or the camera moved since. Save it again and step fully out of view.

My work laptop won't let me turn on Developer mode

On Teams, use the bookmark. On Google Meet, run it from the console.

Privacy

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Dust Out only changes your own outgoing video, only on Teams, Google Meet and sites you turn on yourself. The mic and camera are used only while snap detection is on, and only on your computer.
