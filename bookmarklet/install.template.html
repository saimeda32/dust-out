Dust Out

The bookmark version, for computers where you can't install Chrome extensions.

Dust Out

Drag this button onto your Chrome bookmarks bar. Press Cmd+Shift+B if you can't see the bar.

Works on Microsoft Teams in the browser. Google Meet blocks bookmarks from running, so use the extension there.

Set up (once per call)

  1. Open your meeting in Chrome, not the desktop app.
  2. On the screen before you join, click the Dust Out bookmark. A small card appears at the bottom left. Click it before joining, so it can follow the call if it opens in a separate window.
  3. Pick any background filter you like. You crumble on top of it.
  4. Join with your camera on. The card says You are live and shows a small What others see preview of exactly what your call sends.
  5. Click Save empty room and step fully out of view for 5 seconds. Check the thumbnail shows only the background, then sit back down.
  6. Click Teach my snap and snap 3 times, a second apart. It learns your snap's sound on your mic and remembers it next time.

In the meeting

  1. Snap your fingers, or press Option+Shift+V. You char, crack and blow away as dust.
  2. Stay seated for about 3 seconds while you crumble, then leave. If you get up early it still finishes, using a still of you from the snap.
  3. Sit back down. After 60 seconds the dust flies back and you reform, or snap or press the shortcut to come back sooner.

Optional: snap with your hand on camera

  1. Double-click start-gesture.command in this folder. A Terminal window opens; leave it open during the call.
  2. On the Dust Out card, click Hand gesture. Allow the pop-up and the camera. A small window shows your hand with green dots.
  3. Snap with your hand in view. The small window says Snap! Sent to your meeting and you crumble.

Meeting sites don't allow outside code in their pages, so the hand tracking runs in that small window, served from your own computer. The extension does this on its own and doesn't need the Terminal window.

Keep the camera, lights and background filter the same as when you saved the empty room. Everything runs inside your meeting tab. No video or audio leaves your computer except what the meeting already sends. Reloading the page turns it off.
